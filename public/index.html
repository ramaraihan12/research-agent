research-agent Source on GitHub

Ask a question. Approve the plan. Check the sources.

This agent drafts a short research plan and waits for your approval. Then it searches the web, reads a few pages and writes a report where every claim carries a numbered source.

Review the plan

Nothing is searched until you approve. Edit a line to change what gets looked up.
